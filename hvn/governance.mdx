---
title: 'Governance'
---

HVN is the governance token of Blackhaven. Holders participate in on-chain governance over key protocol parameters and strategic direction. Governance is open to all HVN holders.

Contributors and core participants submit proposals, which HVN holders vote to approve or reject. This structure keeps decision-making efficient while reflecting community input.

### Governance Scope

Proposals may include adjustments to:

| Area                    | Description                                                        |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Treasury Management** | Treasury composition, deployment strategy, and reserve parameters  |
| **Distribution Parameters** | Protocol distribution rates and emission schedules                  |
| **Strategy Approval**   | Approval or removal of MegaETH DeFi strategies                     |
| **MEGA Allocation**     | Allocation of Blackhaven's staked MEGA within the Proximity Market |

### Voting Power

Each HVN token represents one vote. This keeps governance transparent and accessible to all token holders.
