---
title: 'Fee Structure'
---

Blackhaven's fee structure routes protocol revenue to the treasury.

### Revenue Sources

The treasury generates revenue from six primary sources:

1. **Early Exit Fees:** Fees from users who exit before maturity
2. **Backing Arbitrage Module (BAM):** Proceeds routed to the treasury when RBT trades above or below NAV
3. **Sequencer Rewardss:** Variable returns from staking treasury MEGA, where available
4. **RBT Pool Trading Fees:** Fees from the RBT-USDM liquidity pool
5. **HVN Pool Trading Fees:** Fees from the HVN-USDM liquidity pool

### Fee Split and Bootstrap Period

To ensure the treasury has sufficient resources to build a strong foundation, there will be a bootstrap period following the launch of the HVN token.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bootstrap Period">
    At launch, 100% of net treasury revenue is directed to the protocol treasury to build Protocol-Owned Liquidity (POL) and fund operations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-Bootstrap">
    Following the bootstrap period, net treasury revenue continues to accrue to the protocol treasury. Governance may propose mechanisms that direct portions of treasury revenue toward ecosystem operations, liquidity, or protocol uses, which may include HVN-related operations such as buybacks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Governance-Directed Operations">
    Any such mechanisms are subject to HVN governance approval through on-chain proposals. Mechanics, timing, and parameters are determined by governance and are not guaranteed at launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>
