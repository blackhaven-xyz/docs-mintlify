---
title: 'Backing Arbitrage Module (BAM)'
---

The Backing Arbitrage Module (BAM) is a two-way arbitrage mechanism triggered when RBT's market price deviates from its Net Asset Value (NAV). When RBT trades above NAV, existing RBT is sold into the market and proceeds route to the treasury. When RBT trades below NAV, treasury USDm is used to buy and burn RBT.

### How It Works

- **Monitor** — Contract monitors RBT market price against NAV
- **Above NAV** — Sell RBT for USDm, route USDm to reserves
- **Below NAV** — Buy RBT with USDm, burn the RBT
- **Scale** — Operation size scales with deviation from NAV
- **Cooldown** — Cooldown between operations prevents gaming and smooths market impact

## Above NAV (RBT > NAV)

When RBT trades above NAV, the protocol sells existing RBT into the market. USDm proceeds route to the treasury.

## Below NAV (RBT < NAV)

When RBT trades below NAV, the protocol uses treasury USDm to buy RBT from the market and burn it.
